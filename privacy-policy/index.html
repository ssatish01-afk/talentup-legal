Privacy Policy for TalentUp Applications

Effective date: 1 August 2026 Last updated: 1 August 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Satsun (“we”, “us” or “our”), the developer and operator of TalentUp Applications, handles information when you use a TalentUp Application. Where required, separate consent or permission will be obtained within the application before specific information, such as microphone input or answer-sheet images, is processed.

1. Introduction

Satsun develops and operates TalentUp Applications, a family of AI-supported educational and communication-practice tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, when it may be shared, how it is protected and the choices available to users.

2. Applications covered by this policy

This policy currently applies to TalentUp Class10. It will also apply to TalentUp Class11, TalentUp Class12 and other TalentUp educational applications when they are released and expressly link to this policy.

TalentUp Class10 includes curriculum-based Class 10 learning, spoken lessons, AI tutor interactions, quizzes, photographed answer-sheet evaluation, mock interviews, debate practice, group-discussion practice, transcripts and performance feedback.

3. Eligibility and users below 18

TalentUp Applications are intended for users aged 13 and above. Users below 18 should use the service with the knowledge, consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

TalentUp Class10 does not currently collect a verified date of birth or independently verify a parental relationship. A parent or guardian may contact us regarding a minor’s account or information.

4. Information we may collect

Account and session information

  • Firebase user ID, email address, display name derived from the email address and authentication status.
  • A randomly generated device-session identifier, active-device status and relevant timestamps.

TalentUp uses Firebase Authentication for email-and-password sign-in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not intentionally stored in Cloud Firestore by TalentUp.

Learning and preference information

  • Learning track, class or year, subject, chapter, topic and subtopic.
  • Explanation depth, interaction preferences, voice accent and voice-gender preferences.
  • Interview pace, debate stance, difficulty and user-entered practice intent.

User-generated and assessment information

  • Typed questions and answers.
  • Speech converted into transcript text.
  • Learning, interview, debate and group-discussion transcripts.
  • Uploaded answer-sheet photographs and text extracted from them.
  • Quiz results, overall and category scores, strengths, weak areas, corrected answers, missing points and improvement recommendations.
  • Session status, start and completion timestamps and any stop reason, including detected prohibited or abusive language.

5. Information not currently collected directly

TalentUp does not currently collect directly: date of birth or verified age; parent or guardian information; phone number; home or postal address; precise GPS location; contact lists; government identification; school admission number; stored raw microphone recordings; payment-card or bank-account information; or advertising identifiers.

Service providers may independently process limited technical metadata such as IP address, device or network information, timestamps and service logs under their own terms.

6. Camera, microphone and speech features

When a user chooses answer-sheet evaluation, the application may request access to the camera or selected photographs. Uploaded images may be processed to read answers, compare them with questions and marking criteria, generate scores and provide corrections or recommendations.

When a voice feature is used, the application may request microphone access. Device or platform speech-recognition services may convert speech into text. TalentUp does not intentionally store raw microphone recordings.

Users should upload only content they own or are authorised to submit and should avoid including unnecessary personal information about another person.

7. How information is used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; maintain session security; provide lessons and tutor responses; convert text to speech and speech to text; conduct interviews, debates and discussions; evaluate quizzes and answer sheets; generate scores and feedback; save progress, transcripts and results; detect misuse; diagnose technical issues; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Artificial intelligence processing

TalentUp Applications use artificial intelligence to support lesson generation, tutor responses, answer-sheet evaluation, scoring, speech generation and related educational functions.

Information sent for AI processing may include lesson context, subject and topic selections, student questions, relevant transcript portions, answer-sheet images, extracted or written answers, exam questions, marking criteria, voice preferences and text submitted for speech generation.

AI-generated responses may contain errors, inaccuracies or omissions. TalentUp is an educational assistance tool and is not a replacement for teachers or qualified academic guidance. AI-generated content does not guarantee any examination result, interview outcome, employment outcome or academic achievement. Important information should be independently verified.

9. Service providers

  • Google Firebase: application initialization, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and server-side secret management.
  • OpenAI: lesson generation, tutor clarification, answer-sheet reading and evaluation, scoring, feedback and text-to-speech generation.
  • ElevenLabs: selected neural voice features when configured and available.
  • Device or platform speech services: speech recognition and text-to-speech functions.

These providers process information according to their own service terms, privacy policies and contractual safeguards.

10. How information may be shared

Information may be shared with service providers only where reasonably necessary to provide the requested feature, operate the application, maintain security or comply with law.

We may also disclose information where required by valid legal process, to investigate fraud or abuse, to protect users or the public, or during a lawful business restructuring. TalentUp does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers for personalised advertising.

11. Data storage and security

Account, session, transcript and assessment information may be stored using Firebase and related cloud infrastructure. Firestore access is designed so authenticated users can access records associated with their own user ID.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures intended to protect information from unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and security functions, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and complete valid deletion requests.

Learning sessions, transcripts and assessment results may remain available while an account is active so users can review previous activity. Temporary generated voice audio may be written to the device for playback and is not intentionally retained by TalentUp after it is no longer required.

13. Account and data deletion

Users may request deletion of their TalentUp account and associated information by emailing support@satsuntech.com from the registered email address. We may take reasonable steps to verify account ownership before processing the request.

After verification, associated information will be deleted or permanently anonymised, except for limited information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes. Uninstalling the application does not automatically delete the account or stored information.

A separate web-based account-deletion page and an in-app deletion option will be provided before release tracks that require those facilities.

14. User privacy choices

Users may contact us to ask about information associated with their account, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion, withdraw consent where applicable or raise a privacy concern. Some requests may require verification of account ownership.

Users may deny camera or microphone permission, although features requiring those permissions may not function.

15. International processing

Cloud and AI service providers may process information on servers located outside the user’s state, country or region. Where required, reasonable steps are taken to use appropriate contractual and legal safeguards.

16. Payments

TalentUp Class10 does not currently include subscription plans, in-app purchases, Google Play Billing or direct payment-card, bank-account or UPI collection. If paid features are introduced, this policy and the applicable terms will be updated before payment-related information is processed.

17. User responsibilities

Users must not upload, submit or transmit content that is unlawful, abusive, defamatory, obscene or otherwise prohibited; include another person’s personal information without permission; upload confidential examination materials without authorisation; interfere with security controls; or violate applicable law or another person’s rights.

The application may stop or restrict sessions when prohibited or abusive language is detected. Access may be suspended or terminated for serious or repeated misuse.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our applications, service providers, data practices, technology or applicable law.

When we update the policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date shown at the top of the page. If a material change takes effect on a later date, we may also provide a separate effective date and notify users through the application or by email where appropriate.
